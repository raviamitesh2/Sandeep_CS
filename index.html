About R Sandeep & Associates

Dedicated to Your Business Success

R Sandeep & Associates is a leading firm of Company Secretaries and Legal Advisors, committed to providing comprehensive compliance and governance solutions to businesses across Greater Noida and the NCR region.

Established under ICSI (Institute of Company Secretaries of India) regulations, our team specializes in Corporate Compliance, Governance, and Business Registration with a focus on delivering excellence and reliability.

With over 8 years of industry experience, we have successfully served 500+ clients and established ourselves as a trusted partner for startups, SMEs, and established enterprises.
